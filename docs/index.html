The ATTEND study collects clinical records, radiology imaging, IHC, and WES-derived genomic features (TMB, MSI, HRD, aneuploidy, and a per-variant MAF) on the same patients. These reports link the modalities onto a shared patient ID, assemble a per-patient master table, and analyse it.

There is no build order. The join lives in code/build_master.R, and every report calls get_master(), which reads the master table when it exists and builds it when it does not. Any report can be the first one knitted, and wflow_build() with no arguments is always correct.

Each report opens with a four-line scope contract: the question, the cohort, what it reads, and what it deliberately leaves to another report. They are numbered in reading order, 00 to 08.

00 — Methods carries every justification in the pipeline, and the shared definitions each report links to rather than repeating: the two TMB definitions, the aneuploidy classes, what “MMR-deficient only” means, the highlight groups, and how the master is built. Operational recipes — running GISTIC on the cluster, classification alternatives — live in runbooks/.

Build

  • 01 — Data integration · all patients Part 1 translates three ID spaces onto one patient key, collapses each modality to one row per patient, and writes the master. Part 2 audits what that cost: unmapped records, coverage per dataset, the UpSet of who has which modality, and value sanity.

QC and validation

  • 02 — FlowPath vs the pathologist · all imaged Does the automated quantification agree with the pathologist’s eye, on continuous measures and categorical calls.
  • 03 — Covariate distributions by MMR status · all patients ECDF and Kolmogorov–Smirnov per continuous covariate, plus the aneuploidy score itself contrasted between MMR-deficient and -proficient tumours.

Findings

  • 04 — Survival · all patients Every Kaplan–Meier and Cox model in the pipeline: treatment arm within panels defined by aneuploidy × MMR, then TMB, HRD, MSI, and immune content.
  • 05 — Aneuploidy in MMR-deficient tumours · MMRd only Part 1 takes aneuploidy against mutation status, one gene at a time. Part 2 takes it against cell-type composition under three normalizations.
  • 06 — Treatment response and cell-type composition · all patients + MMRd The composition panels of report 05 Part 2, re-keyed on treatment response — a 6-month landmark on the survival endpoint, with patients censored before the landmark reported as unclassifiable rather than counted as failures. Every figure is drawn for the whole cohort and for the MMR-deficient stratum, and one interaction model tests whether they differ.
  • 07 — Mutation landscape and copy number · all patients Part 1 oncoplots and the TCGA Fig. 2d subgroup frequency comparison. Part 2 recurrent copy number at arm and genome-wide resolution. Part 3 the aneuploidy × MMR peak contrast.
  • 08 — Tumour mutational burden · all patients Part 1 asks whether TMB resolves the hypermutator population as a genuine second mode. Part 2 draws TMB by aneuploidy once per MMR stratum. Part 3 replicates the whole battery in TCGA UCEC, which is tumour-normal paired.

External

  • 09 — TCGA integrated classification · all patients Reproduces the TCGA endometrial cascade (Kandoth et al. 2013), clustering GISTIC thresholded peaks with 1 − Pearson distance and Ward.D2 linkage and sweeping k = 2–8 on one chromosome heatmap. The serous-like call is directional; the undirected call is printed alongside and [DISAGREE] flagged when they differ.

Conventions

Shared definitions are written once, in 00-methods, and linked from the reports that use them rather than rendered into each one. Reports show derivations and results, and carry no takeaway or interpretation sections: conclusions belong in the manuscript, where they can be revised as the data changes.

Every chunk that produces a figure or table is preceded by a derivation note saying what is shown, where it comes from in the raw data, and which operations change its interpretation.

code/tests/test_rmd_style.R enforces the structure and code/tests/test_rmd_parse.R checks every chunk parses. Both are base R, so they run without tidyverse or the cohort.

Configuration lives in one place

  • code/build_master.R — the join, the crosswalk assembly, and get_master().
  • code/attend_harmonise.R — ID spaces, the two crosswalks, the collapse-to-patient rule.
  • code/attend_classes.R — column names (attend_cols), thresholds, class derivation, gene panels, the TMB definitions and labels, and the CNV/TCGA-cascade config.
  • code/attend_plots.R — shared palettes, attend_theme(), the highlight overlay.
  • code/attend_ihc.R — FlowPath IHC metric definitions (reports 02, 04, 05, 06).
  • code/attend_scna.R — GISTIC peak union, panel score, permutation and LOO stability.
  • code/attend_bimodality.R — tmb_long(), tmb_battery(), and the bimodality measures.
